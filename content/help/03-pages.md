# Pages: rearrange, merge and split

The **Pages** screen shows every page as a picture. Changes you make here go into the new file when you save; the file you opened isn't changed.

## Choosing pages

- Click a page to choose it. `Ctrl`+click adds or removes a page, and `Shift`+click chooses a run of pages.
- `Ctrl+A` chooses every page.

## Changing pages

- **Rotate left** and **Rotate right** turn the chosen pages.
- **Delete** removes them. A PDF needs at least one page, so the last page can't be deleted.
- **Move earlier** and **Move later** move them one place, or drag them to where you want them.
- **Insert blank page** adds an empty page after the chosen one, the same size.
- **Undo** and **Redo** step back and forward through your changes.

## Merging files

Click **Add files…** and choose one or more PDFs, or drag them onto the pages. Their pages are added at the end. When you save, each file's bookmarks are kept under a bookmark named after the file.

## Saving

- **Save as new file…** saves all the pages as they are now.
- **Save selected pages…** saves just the chosen pages, and keeps your document open as it is.
- **Split…** saves several files into a folder you choose: every page as its own file, every few pages, or the page ranges you type (such as `1-3, 5, 7-9`).

Links and bookmarks that pointed to pages left out of a new file are removed, and the message after saving says how many. See [Saving and your original files](07-saving.md).

## Keyboard

Click a page first, then:

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move between pages |
| `Space` | Choose or unchoose the page |
| `Shift` + arrow keys | Choose a run of pages |
| `Alt+Left`, `Alt+Right` | Move the chosen pages |
| `Delete` | Delete the chosen pages |
| `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y` | Undo, redo |
