# Annotating

On the **Document** screen, with **Annotate** chosen at the start of the second toolbar, you can mark up the file. Annotations are saved as standard PDF annotations, each with its own look, so other readers such as Microsoft Edge and Adobe Acrobat show the same thing.

Pick a colour from the six circles in the toolbar before you start. Each tool has its own choices beside the colours.

## Marking text

Choose **Highlight**, **Underline** or **Strikeout**, then select the text with the mouse.

## Notes and text boxes

- Choose **Note**, then click on the page where the note goes, and type.
- Choose **Text box**, pick a **Text size**, then click where the text goes, and type. An empty text box isn't saved.

## Drawing and shapes

- Choose **Draw** and draw on the page with the mouse. Each stroke joins the same drawing until you choose another tool. Pick a **Thickness** first.
- Choose **Shape**, pick a **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** or **Arrow**, and drag across the page. Rectangles and ellipses can be filled with a lighter shade of their colour: tick **Fill**.

## Stamps

Choose **Stamp**, pick one (**Approved**, **Reviewed**, **Draft**, **Confidential**, **Not approved**, or **Your own text**), and click where it goes. Each stamp shows today's date under it.

## Changing, moving and removing

With **Select** chosen, click an annotation. A panel opens where you can change its colour, its comment or text, its thickness, fill or text size, or **Delete** it. You can also press `Delete`.

Notes, text boxes, drawings, shapes and stamps can be moved and resized:

- Drag one to move it.
- Drag one of the small squares at its corners (or at a line's ends) to resize it.
- Or use the keys: the arrow keys move it, `Shift` with an arrow moves it further, and `Alt` with an arrow makes it larger or smaller.

`Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y` undo and redo, and `Escape` goes back to **Select**.

## Annotations already in the file

Annotations someone else added show as they always did. Click one with **Select** to see who made it and when, and to:

- change its comment;
- change its colour (highlights, underlines, strikeouts, notes, drawings, lines and text boxes);
- reply to it;
- **Delete** it. It's then left out of the new file completely, with its replies.

Anything you don't change stays exactly as it was, including its author and date.

## Comments and replies

Click **Comments** in the toolbar (or press `Ctrl+Shift+M`) to see every annotation in the file, old and new, page by page, with who made it and its comment. Choose a person or a kind of annotation to see only those. Click one to go to it.

To answer a comment, select it and click **Reply**. Replies show under the comment they answer, here and in other readers. An empty reply isn't saved.

## Burning annotations into the page

An annotation burned into the page keeps its look but becomes part of the page, so nobody can change or remove it. Its comment and replies aren't kept.

- To burn in one annotation, select it and tick **Burn into the page when saving**.
- To burn in all of them, open **Comments** and tick **When saving, burn all annotations into the pages**. Links and form fields aren't affected.

## Your name

Other readers show who wrote each annotation. To put your name on them, type it in **Settings**, under **Annotations**. If you leave it empty, no name is added.

## Saving

Click **Save as new file…** (or press `Ctrl+S`). Your annotations and changes go into the new file with the pages, arranged as on the **Pages** screen. Annotations on pages you've deleted there are left out, and the message after saving says so. The file you opened isn't changed.
