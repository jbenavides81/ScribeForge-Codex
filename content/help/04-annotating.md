# Annotating

On the **Document** screen, with **Annotate** chosen at the start of the second toolbar, you can mark up the file. Annotations are saved as standard PDF annotations, so other readers such as Microsoft Edge and Adobe Acrobat show them too.

## Marking text

Choose **Highlight**, **Underline** or **Strikeout**, then select the text with the mouse. Pick a colour first from the six circles in the toolbar.

## Notes and text boxes

- Choose **Note**, then click on the page where the note goes, and type.
- Choose **Text box**, then click where the text goes, and type. An empty text box isn't saved.

## Changing or removing an annotation

With **Select** chosen, click an annotation. A panel opens where you can change its colour, add a comment or change its text, or **Delete** it. You can also press `Delete`.

`Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y` undo and redo, and `Escape` goes back to **Select**.

## Your name

Other readers show who wrote each annotation. To put your name on them, type it in **Settings**, under **Annotations**. If you leave it empty, no name is added.

## Saving

Click **Save as new file…** (or press `Ctrl+S`). Your annotations go into the new file with the pages, arranged as on the **Pages** screen. Annotations on pages you've deleted there are left out, and the message after saving says so.

ScribeForge only changes annotations you've added since opening the file. Annotations that were already in the file are kept as they are.
