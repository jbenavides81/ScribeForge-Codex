# Code of conduct

ScribeForge is for everyone. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) is asked to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- keep to the subject: the work, not the person;
- welcome people who are new, and explain things plainly;
- respect people's privacy: never post someone's personal details or private documents;
- take "no" for an answer, about a change or anything else.

Not welcome here: insults, harassment, threats, unwanted sexual attention, discrimination of any kind, and posting someone's private information.

## If something goes wrong

Use GitHub's **Report content** option (the **…** menu on any comment, issue or pull request). You can send the report to the maintainer, or to GitHub. The maintainer may edit or hide comments, lock a conversation, and block someone from the project, for as long as needed.
